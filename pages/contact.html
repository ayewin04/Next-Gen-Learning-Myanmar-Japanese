Frequently Asked Questions

📚 Is this platform completely free?
Yes! Next Gen Learning Myanmar is 100% free. All vocabulary, grammar, kanji, conversations, and mock tests are available at no cost.
📖 Can I download the PDF materials?
Absolutely! All vocabulary lists, grammar guides, and kanji sheets are available as downloadable PDF files. You can print them for offline study.
🔊 Do you provide audio for pronunciation?
Yes! Each vocabulary word, grammar example, and conversation includes Japanese audio with native pronunciation.
🎯 How many mock tests are available?
We offer 3 full mock tests for each JLPT level (N5, N4, N3, N2, N1) - that's 15 complete practice tests with timers and scoring!
🌏 Do you support Burmese language?
Yes! Our platform is designed for Myanmar learners. All content includes both English and Burmese explanations.
📱 Is this mobile-friendly?
Yes! Our website is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and computers.
