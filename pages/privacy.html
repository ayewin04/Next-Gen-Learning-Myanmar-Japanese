Privacy Policy

Welcome to Next Gen Learning Myanmar. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Japanese learning platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide you with the best learning experience:

  • Learning Progress: Your test scores, completed lessons, and mastered vocabulary (stored locally on your device)
  • Usage Data: Which JLPT levels you study and how you interact with flashcards and mock tests
  • Device Information: Browser type, language preference, and screen size for optimal display

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To track your learning progress across N5 to N1 levels
  • To save your test results and mastered cards
  • To improve our content and user experience
  • To provide personalized recommendations for your JLPT preparation

3. Data Storage

All your learning data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. We do NOT collect, store, or share your personal information on any external servers. This means:

  • ✅ Your progress stays on your device
  • ✅ No account registration required
  • ✅ You can clear your data anytime by clearing browser cache
  • ✅ No third-party tracking or analytics

4. Downloadable PDF Resources

The vocabulary, grammar, and kanji PDF files available on our platform are for personal educational use only. You may download and print them for your JLPT studies, but redistribution or commercial use is prohibited.

5. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to external resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites.

6. Children's Privacy

Our platform is suitable for learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our Contact Page.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page.

By using Next Gen Learning Myanmar, you agree to this privacy policy.
